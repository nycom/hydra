## Eval gaps
- The delta validator's full-range test guard re-raised a reviewer finding under the reviewer's id, not a `v1-R*` id. The S17 rule scores only `v1-R*` ids. If a reviewer files W3 at minor and later drops it, the guard's re-raise has no scored id. The rule should say that a guard re-raise of a `c1-F*` W3 finding counts as `alsoCaughtBy` `test-guard`.
- No scenario covers a stage result with a placeholder or non-commit `sha`. A workflow self-test (not a live eval) should feed the docs step a non-hash sha and expect the tip check from Change 1.
